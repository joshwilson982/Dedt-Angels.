THE PERSON BEHIND THE IDEA
JW

Meet Josh Wilson

Founder & Creator of Debt Angels

Debt Angels was created around a simple idea: people who want to help should be able to connect with people who need relief from debt.

The vision is to bring together direct giving and a community fund, with priority for families experiencing financial hardship.

Our goal is to make generosity easier to understand, and to build a platform grounded in transparency, dignity, and trust.

We're building this together.

Debt Angels is currently a demonstration, not a live fundraising or debt-payment service. All requests and donations in this prototype are fictional or simulated. No real funds are collected or sent to creditors.
