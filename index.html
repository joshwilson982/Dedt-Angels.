THE PERSON BEHIND THE IDEA

Meet Josh Wilson

Founder & Creator of Debt Angels

Debt Angels was created around a simple idea: people who want to help should be able to connect with people who need relief from debt.

The vision combines direct giving and a community fund, prioritizing families experiencing hardship and emphasizing compassion, transparency, and dignity.

This is a prototype, not a live fundraising or creditor-payment service.
